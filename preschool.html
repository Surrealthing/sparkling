Our Preschool Approach

Learning that begins with wonder

We believe young children learn best through play, curiosity, and meaningful experiences. Our days balance free play, guided exploration, and structured activities, giving children both the freedom to discover and the support to grow.

Free Play

Unstructured time where children follow their curiosity, build friendships, and direct their own discovery.

Guided Exploration

Facilitators gently prompt, question, and scaffold. Learning deepens without taking over.

Structured Activity

Purposeful group experiences that build language, problem-solving, and routines for confidence.

How Children Learn Here

Four spaces, many languages of learning

Our classrooms are thoughtfully designed into distinct learning zones that invite children to engage through every sense and strength.

Imagination
Stories, pretend play, and creation

Where children become doctors, shopkeepers, astronauts, and authors. Pretend play is the workshop for thinking in symbols.

Exploration
Hands-on discovery and problem-solving

Science tables, loose parts, and sensory play. Children test theories with their hands and trust the questions behind them.

Interaction
Conversation, collaboration, community

Small-group work, circle time, peer problem-solving. Children learn to listen, negotiate, and belong to each other.

Movement
Physical play and body awareness

Climbing, running, dancing, balance. The body is how young children think — and we make room for that thinking every day.

More Than Academics

At this age, how they grow matters most

What a 3-year-old memorises will fade. How they learn to understand themselves, regulate emotions, build trust, and stay curious stays with them for life.

These early skills form the foundation for everything that follows, reading, writing, friendship, resilience, and the quiet belief that "I can figure this out."

Feel
Understand and express emotions with the vocabulary to name them.
Believe
Build confidence and independence through real ownership of small choices.
Voice
Develop the language to talk about feelings, ideas, and what they're noticing.
See
Become aware of themselves, and of others, with empathy as the starting point.

Our Role as Educators

Facilitators of learning, not givers of answers

Our teachers do not see themselves as knowledge-bearers or instruction-givers. They design environments and ask the questions that help children reach their own insights.

Carefully observe each child

Pay close attention to what lights a child up and where they struggle.

Listen to ideas and questions

Treat children's thinking as real thinking, worth responding to.

Document learning journeys

Photos, notes, and artefacts that make growth visible to children and parents.

Design from children's interests

Next week's plan emerges from this week's wonderings. Curriculum follows curiosity.

Age Groups

A place for every little one

Each age group has its own rhythm, materials, and routines — designed to match where children are and gently invite them forward.

Guppies
1.5+ · Pre-Playgroup

First steps into a learning community, gentle routines, sensory play, and trust.

Pups
2+ · Playgroup

Social play, language bursts, and the beginning of expressing what they want and feel.

Calves
3+ · Nursery

Problem-solving, pretend play, and the roots of literacy and numeracy through stories.

Cubs
4+ · Juniors

Collaborating, leading, and taking on small responsibilities that build self-trust.

Joeys
5+ · Seniors

School-readiness with depth: independence, love of learning, and their own strong voice.

Our Promise

What we will and will not do

Parents deserve to know exactly what kind of partner we intend to be, and what we refuse to be.

WE WILL

Seed a lifelong love for learning in your child

Instil a strong sense of community and civic sense

Develop the useful skills that make your child school-ready

Nurture emotional and social intelligence alongside academics

Empower you to work alongside your child at home

Ensure your child has fun while learning, every single day

Make it an experience you and your child cherish

WE WILL NOT

Chase outcomes at the expense of the learning process

Complain about your child's focus, we'll build it, with you

Complain about their interest, we'll discover what sparks them

Complain about social challenges, we'll work through them as partners

Complain about reluctance to write, we'll nurture it patiently

Reduce your child to a report card or a milestone chart
