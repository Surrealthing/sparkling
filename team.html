Our Team

The people, and the milestones, behind the school.

Four things we hold ourselves to, every single day.

We see the whole child

Beyond academics, thinking, emotions, confidence, and character all matter equally in how we teach.

Facilitators, not instructors

Instead of giving answers, we guide children to ask better questions and discover their own thinking.

We are constantly growing

Always reflecting, learning, and evolving, so we can show up better for the children in our care.

We work with parents

Parents are partners in this journey. Together, we shape an environment where every child can thrive.

Leadership

Meet the people leading Sparkling Mindz.

Founder & CEO

Sreeja Iyer

An educator and entrepreneur with over 20 years of experience, Sreeja has shaped the learning journeys of more than 10,000 children across Bengaluru. A gold medallist from Anna University with a Master's in Learning & Teaching from the University of Oxford, she founded Sparkling Mindz in 2010 with a single conviction: children can learn deeply, joyfully, and for life when education is designed around them.

She is the architect of IBCL — the Innovation Beyond Connected Learning framework that runs through every classroom at Sparkling Mindz, from preschool through A-Levels.

Chief Mentor & Head of Operations

Minesh Happy

Minesh brings a deep understanding of child development, facilitation, and school operations to Sparkling Mindz. With years of experience mentoring learners and building teacher capacity, he plays a central role in shaping how our classrooms work day to day — from the rhythm of a school morning to how a moment of friction becomes a moment of learning.

A thoughtful presence in the school, Minesh partners closely with facilitators, parents, and children to keep the whole ecosystem aligned with what matters most: children who learn to think, feel, and act with intention.
